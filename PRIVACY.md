# Privacy

Z ULT Connect does **not** collect, store or send any personal data.

- It has no account, no analytics and no telemetry, and it never connects to the internet.
- It talks only to your headphones, over Bluetooth, on your own PC.
- Your settings and a troubleshooting log are saved locally in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Z ULT Connect`.
  The log can contain the names of songs you play (sent by the headphones) and stays on your computer
  unless you choose to share it.
- Uninstalling the app and deleting that folder removes everything.

Questions? Open an issue on this repository.
